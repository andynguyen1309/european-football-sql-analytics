# Theme 1 — Club and League Performance

## Business objective

Evaluate club performance, consistency, home–away strength, scoring output and performance relative to estimated player market value across the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A and Ligue 1 from 2020–21 to 2024–25.

## Scope and methodology

The analysis covers 8,982 domestic league matches and 486 club-season records. Five-season comparisons include only clubs that appeared in all five seasons, producing a consistent comparison group of 63 clubs. Seasonal rankings use win percentage, with points per match and goal difference as supporting measures. Market-value efficiency is measured as the difference between actual and expected points per match within each league-season, where expected performance is estimated from the natural logarithm of participating-player market value. Club-seasons require at least 80% valuation coverage and valuations no more than 180 days old.

## Executive summary

Manchester City recorded the strongest overall five-season win percentage at 70.00%, narrowly ahead of Paris Saint-Germain at 69.78%. Bayer Leverkusen produced the most dominant single league-season, winning 82.35% of its Bundesliga matches in 2023–24. Bayern Munich led the five-season attacking comparison with 2.829 goals per match, while Real Madrid had the strongest defensive record at 0.837 goals conceded per match. Paris Saint-Germain produced the best away win percentage at 68.13%, whereas Bayern Munich led at home with 76.47%.

The largest year-on-year improvements belonged to Bayer Leverkusen and VfB Stuttgart from 2022–23 to 2023–24, each increasing points per match by 1.18. Girona experienced the sharpest subsequent decline, falling by 1.05 points per match in 2024–25. In value-adjusted terms, Clermont Foot’s 2022–23 season produced the largest positive performance residual, while RC Lens had the strongest average residual among clubs with at least three eligible seasons. The Bundesliga was the highest-scoring league overall at 3.135 goals per match.

## Limitations

- Win percentage and points per match do not adjust for schedule strength, injuries, managerial changes or tactical context.
- Cross-league comparisons do not account for differences in competitive balance.
- Five-season analysis excludes promoted or relegated clubs that did not appear in every season.
- Market value is an estimate and does not equal wage cost, transfer expenditure or accounting squad value.
- The efficiency model measures association and relative outperformance; it does not establish causation.
- Player valuation coverage and timing rules reduce missingness risk but cannot eliminate estimation error.
